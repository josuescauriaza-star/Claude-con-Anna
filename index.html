About the Event

What is the Genuine Cup?

The Genuine Cup is an inclusive competition organized by the Genuine Foundation that celebrates neurodiverse talent. We bring together individuals from all walks of life to compete, connect, and showcase their unique abilities in a supportive environment.

Community

Be part of a welcoming community that values diversity and celebrates every individual's strengths.

Impact

Your time and skills directly contribute to creating opportunities for neurodiverse individuals to thrive.

Experience

Gain meaningful volunteer experience while working alongside passionate people dedicated to inclusion.
